I want you to design a parameterized SPI Master controller in synthesizable SystemVerilog.
Before writing any RTL, think through the design step by step:

1. Identify all inputs and outputs (clk, rst, start, tx_data, miso, sclk, mosi, cs_n, rx_data, busy, done) and parameters (DATA_WIDTH, CLK_DIV, CPOL, CPHA).
2. Define the FSM states: Idle, Assert CS, Shift, Deassert CS, Done, and what each state drives (cs_n level, sclk toggling, shift enable).
3. Define how CPOL and CPHA affect behavior:
   - CPOL: idle level of sclk (0 or 1)
   - CPHA: whether data is driven on the first or second clock edge, and sampled on the opposite edge
   - Derive the four mode combinations (Mode 0-3) as specific edge conditions for "drive mosi" vs "sample miso"
4. Define the datapath: shift register width (DATA_WIDTH), bit counter, sclk generation via CLK_DIV, and how tx_data is loaded and rx_data is assembled bit-by-bit.
5. Identify corner cases: start asserted while busy, cs_n timing relative to the first and last sclk edge (setup/hold), DATA_WIDTH values other than 8 (16/32), CLK_DIV = 1 (fastest possible sclk), and switching CPOL/CPHA between transactions.
6. Define reset behavior (synchronous reset, cs_n deasserted/high, sclk at idle level per CPOL, FSM to Idle).
7. Only after completing the above steps, generate the synthesizable SystemVerilog RTL implementing this SPI Master.

Show your reasoning for each step before presenting the final RTL code.
