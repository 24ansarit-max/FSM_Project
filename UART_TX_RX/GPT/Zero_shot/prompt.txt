Design a parameterized UART (serial communication protocol) controller in synthesizable SystemVerilog, containing a transmitter and a receiver, each driven by an FSM.

Transmitter FSM states: Idle, Start, Data, Parity, Stop, Done.
Receiver FSM states: Idle, Start Detect, Data, Parity, Stop, Error/Done.

Requirements:
- Parameters: CLK_FREQ_HZ, BAUD_RATE, DATA_BITS (7 or 8), PARITY_MODE (none / even / odd), STOP_BITS (1 or 2).
- Inputs: clk, rst, tx_start, tx_data[DATA_BITS-1:0], rx_serial_in.
- Outputs: tx_serial_out, tx_busy, tx_done, rx_data[DATA_BITS-1:0], rx_valid, framing_error, parity_error, overrun_error.
- Include a baud-rate generator derived from the clock frequency and baud rate parameters.
- The receiver must sample each bit near its midpoint and handle a false start bit (glitch on the line).
- Handle: tx_start asserted while busy, a missing stop bit (framing error), a parity mismatch, and a new byte arriving before the previous one was read (overrun).
- Provide the complete RTL code and explain the design, including the FSM state transitions and the baud/sampling logic.
