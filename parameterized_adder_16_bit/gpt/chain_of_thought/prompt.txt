Define the required operations (addition, with optional signed/unsigned mode).
Define the datapath operations (bit-width of intermediate signals, carry propagation).
Identify corner cases (all-ones overflow, carry-in with max operands, signed overflow, WIDTH=1 edge case).
Determine reset behavior (if registered outputs are used, specify synchronous/asynchronous reset).

Specification → Operations → Widths → Signedness → Overflow → RTL

Then generate synthesizable SystemVerilog for a parameterized 16-bit adder (default WIDTH = 16) based on this analysis."
